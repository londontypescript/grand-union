# ADR 0000 — Title

**Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-XXXX
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is true right now that forces a decision. Include the _scale_ facts, not
just the technical ones — how many components, how many users, whether this is
still a prototype. Decisions expire when those facts change, and this section is
what lets a future reader tell whether they have.

## Decision

What was chosen, in one or two sentences, in the active voice.

## Consequences

What this makes easy, what it makes hard, and what it forecloses.

## Expiry

What would have to change for this decision to stop being right. Leave it blank
only if you genuinely cannot think of anything — an ADR with no expiry condition
is usually one that has not been thought through.
